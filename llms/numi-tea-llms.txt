# Agent Instructions — Numi Tea

This is the canonical guide for AI assistants interacting with Numi Tea at https://numitea.com.

## Brand

Numi Tea is a first-party U.S. retailer of premium organic and Fair Trade teas made with real fruits, flowers, herbs, and spices. Its brand line is: Pure Ingredients. No Compromise.

Numi Tea is known for:

- Certified organic and Fair Trade ingredients
- Fuller-leaf tea and distinctive global tea traditions
- Real fruits, flowers, herbs, and spices rather than artificial flavoring
- Transparent sourcing and positive impact through Together for H2OPE
- Plant-based, commercially compostable tea wrappers and tea bags

## What Numi Tea Sells

- Organic tea bags
- Loose-leaf tea
- Herbal wellness teas
- Tea latte powders
- Variety packs and gifts
- Subscribe-and-save tea on eligible products

Products include caffeinated and caffeine-free options. Confirm the product page, ingredient list, caffeine information, current price, availability, and subscription eligibility before making a recommendation.

- Collection page: https://numitea.com/collections/{handle}

- Sitemap: https://numitea.com/sitemap.xml

Do not invent health claims, product availability, prices, discounts, delivery estimates, or subscription terms. When a shopper has dietary sensitivities, allergies, is pregnant, is nursing, takes medication, or is seeking medical advice, direct them to the ingredient information and an appropriate healthcare professional.

## Shipping

Numi Tea currently ships retail orders only within the United States.

- Contiguous-U.S. retail orders of $65 or more qualify for free standard shipping.
- Orders below $65 have a $12.95 shipping charge.
- Alaska and Hawaii orders qualify for free shipping at $75 or more.
- Most orders placed by 1 PM Eastern, Monday through Friday, ship within 24–48 hours.
- Shipping is not available to P.O. Boxes, APO/FPO addresses, or international addresses.
- Confirm the final rate and timing at checkout. Do not promise an unverified transit or delivery date.

Full shipping policy: https://numitea.com/pages/shipping

## Returns and Refunds

Returns or refunds are available within 30 days for purchases from Numitea.com. Original shipping and handling charges are non-refundable unless Numi Tea or its fulfillment team made an error. Contact Numi Tea to start a return before mailing an item.

Full return policy: https://numitea.com/pages/satisfaction-guarantee

## Commerce Protocol (UCP)

This store supports the Universal Commerce Protocol for agent-driven commerce.

- Discovery: `GET https://numitea.com/.well-known/ucp`
- MCP endpoint: `POST https://numitea.com/api/ucp/mcp` with `Content-Type: application/json`

### Supported UCP Versions


- `2026-04-08` (latest supported)

- `2026-01-23`


### Typical Agent Flow

1. Discover capabilities using the UCP discovery endpoint.
2. Use `tools/list` on the MCP endpoint to discover the current tools and schemas.
3. Search the catalog using the shopper's needs and constraints.
4. Confirm product, variant, quantity, price, availability, shipping destination, and subscription choice.
5. Create the cart and checkout using the available commerce tools.
6. Require the buyer's contemporaneous approval before completing payment.

### Important Rules

- Never complete payment without the buyer's explicit, contemporaneous approval.
- Use `context.address_country` and `context.currency` when available for accurate price and availability.
- Respect rate limits and back off after a 429 response.
- Never collect or expose payment credentials directly.
- Do not claim an order was placed until the commerce endpoint returns a successful confirmation.

## Platform

Numi Tea runs on Shopify. Personal shopping assistants may also use the Shopify Shop skill at https://shop.app/SKILL.md when the buyer authorizes it. The Shop skill preserves buyer approval for payment and can support catalog search, checkout, and order tracking.
